AnyEssens Automation

AnyEssens Automation is a private, single-user desktop application used by the owner of the AnyEssens YouTube channel. It helps prepare, upload, schedule and analyse YouTube Shorts.

What the application does

The application connects to YouTube only after the channel owner grants access. It can upload and schedule videos, read channel and video metadata, and retrieve YouTube Analytics reports. Any management action is performed for the connected channel and under the account owner's control.

Data handling

OAuth credentials and generated media are stored locally on the account owner's computer. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to data brokers.
