Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

This policy explains how AnyEssens Automation accesses and uses Google and YouTube data. The application is a private, single-user desktop tool operated by the owner of the connected YouTube channel.

Data accessed

  • YouTube channel identity and basic channel information.
  • Video metadata, captions, ratings and comments where required for channel management.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the connected channel.
  • Authorization tokens provided by Google OAuth.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to upload and schedule videos, manage the connected channel when requested by its owner, and review video performance. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens, generated media and automation records are stored locally on the account owner's computer. The application does not operate a central user database and does not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data may pass through Google APIs only as required to provide the requested YouTube functionality.

Retention and deletion

Local tokens and automation records are retained until the account owner deletes them or disconnects the application. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account connections page. After revocation, locally stored OAuth tokens can be deleted from the application configuration.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AnyEssens Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the public project issue tracker.
